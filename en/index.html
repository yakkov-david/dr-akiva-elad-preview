Expertise that starts at the root

From a single implant to full-mouth rehabilitation, with the world's most advanced technology in experienced hands.

  • Immediate-loading implants

    Implants and provisional teeth are placed on the same day. You leave the clinic with a complete, stable and aesthetic smile, with no toothless waiting period.

  • Bone grafting & regeneration

    Sinus lift, socket preservation and ridge reconstruction, using graft materials and techniques Dr. Elad develops and teaches in Israel and abroad.

  • Aesthetics & oral rehabilitation

    Porcelain veneers, zirconia crowns and full rehabilitation, digitally designed so you can see the result before we begin.

  • Teeth whitening

    Professional, safe in-clinic whitening for a brighter, natural result after a single session.

  • Advanced surgery

    Surgical solutions for complex cases, by a surgeon with more than three decades of experience.

  • Hygiene & prevention

    Professional cleaning, guidance and ongoing maintenance, because your new smile should stay that way for years.
