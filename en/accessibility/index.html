Accessibility Statement

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Our commitment

At Dr. Akiva Elad's clinic we are committed to providing equal, respectful and accessible service to all patients, including people with disabilities.

This website was designed in accordance with the Israeli Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities Law (1998), the Accessibility Regulations for Services (2013), and Israeli Standard IS 5568, which is based on WCAG 2.0 level AA.

Accessibility features

  • The site is available in Hebrew (right-to-left) and English, and each page declares its language for screen readers.
  • Semantic structure of headings, landmarks and links for comfortable screen-reader navigation.
  • Full keyboard navigation, with a clear visible focus indicator.
  • A “Skip to main content” link at the top of the page.
  • Alternative text for meaningful images; decorative images are hidden from screen readers.
  • High color contrast between text and background.
  • Responsive layout that supports browser text zoom up to 200% without loss of content.
  • All animations are disabled automatically for users who enabled “reduce motion” in their operating system.
  • Form fields have clear labels and status messages.

Third-party content

The site includes third-party content such as Google Maps, Google reviews, and links to WhatsApp and Waze. Some of these components may not be fully accessible. We strive to choose providers that take accessibility seriously.

Despite our efforts, some parts of the site may not yet be fully accessible. If you encounter any difficulty, please let us know and we will work to fix it promptly.

Clinic accessibility

For details about physical accessibility at the clinic (building access, elevator, parking) and to arrange any special accommodations for your visit, please contact us by phone or email.
