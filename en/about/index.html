Our clinic & team

Dr. Akiva Elad, the technology in our clinic, and the people who will be with you all the way.

Dr. Akiva Elad

Implantology, surgery & dental aesthetics

A graduate of the Hebrew University–Hadassah School of Dental Medicine in Jerusalem (1989), Dr. Elad is one of the pioneers of immediate loading in Israel and worldwide, with over 30 years of experience in implants, bone grafting and advanced surgery.

He serves on the board of the Israeli Association of Oral Implantology (IAOI), develops new treatment methods and bone graft materials, and lectures at conferences in Israel and abroad. In his Tel Aviv clinic he leads a fully digital approach: 3D scanning, computer-aided planning and CAD-CAM production.

"My goal is simple: that you leave here with a smile you are proud of, as quickly as possible."
  1. 1989DMD, Hebrew University–Hadassah, Jerusalem

  4. 2023Lead author of two papers on bone regeneration with magnesium membranes
